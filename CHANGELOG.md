## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The draft overlay: a panel beside Arena listing the pack you're picking from, ranked by 17Lands
  GIH WR, with art, colours, rarity, a grade against the set's mean, a card preview on hover, and a
  choice of whose games the win rates come from.
- The deck overlay: what's left in your library while you play, card by card, with the chance your
  next draw is each one.
- Either overlay folds away into a small tab on Arena's right edge when it is in the way of the
  game, and opens again with a click.
- Your drafts and games saved on your Mac, one file per day.
- Uploads to 17Lands, once you give it your token. Without one, nothing leaves your Mac.
- A menu-bar icon, a window with the log of what Brainstorm did, and Settings.
